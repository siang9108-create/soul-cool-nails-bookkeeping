Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Soulcool Daily Multi handles information needed to provide salon management features.

Information we process

Soulcool Daily Multi processes information needed to operate a salon, which may include store names, employee names, store codes, appointment information, service amounts, payment methods, tips, and reporting data.

How information is used

We use this information to provide app login, employee management, appointment, checkout, revenue summary, and reporting features.

Data sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information, and we do not provide user information to advertisers.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services such as Apple TestFlight, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, and the Square API when enabled. Information handled by those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect operational and account information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Support and data assistance

Users may request support or assistance with questions related to their data through the support options provided by Soulcool DM.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app and its services evolve. The latest version will be posted on this page.
