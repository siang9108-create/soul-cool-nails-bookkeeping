Soulcool Daily Multi 隱私政策
營運者：Soulcool
生效日期：2026-10-22

1. 適用範圍與資料用途

本政策適用於Soulcool Daily Multi及相關支援服務。我們處理店家與使用者識別、姓名、角色、帳號狀態、登入驗證資訊及Session，以提供登入、本人驗證與帳號管理。依啟用功能，亦處理員工資料、選用員工頭像、預約及設計圖片、預約、顧客姓名與備註，以及服務金額、折扣、小費、付款方式分配、每日收入、業績與結算資料，用於店內營運、結帳、報表及對帳。使用者主動提供的支援訊息用於回覆及處理問題。

2. 員工帳號及存取

Employee帳號由Owner建立及管理，使用個人帳號與PIN登入。Owner可以記帳、結帳、管理員工及查看報表；Employee僅能查看授權資料，不得新增、修改或刪除帳務。店家應確認輸入資料及授權的適當性。

3. Google、Square、網站及資料使用

App向Google Apps Script後端傳送登入驗證、Session、員工管理、預約、圖片、記帳、結帳與報表等功能所需的請求；既有服務使用Google Sheets處理店家資料。店家連接Square時，App可顯示經授權的預約及相關營運資訊。記錄付款金額及付款方式，不代表App本身處理付款卡交易。
目前App未整合第三方廣告或分析SDK，未實作以IDFA／IDFV為基礎的廣告識別或跨App廣告追蹤，也未發現App自行上傳分析事件的功能。App有本機診斷紀錄，不能將本機紀錄等同遠端分析事件。
官方網站由GitHub Pages提供。開啟隱私政策時，瀏覽器會連接該網站；App連結不附帶帳號、Store ID、PIN或Session Token。目前公開首頁、隱私政策及Support頁未嵌入已辨識的分析或廣告追蹤程式；首頁腳本僅更新頁尾年份。
Apple提供App發布及適用的測試服務。Google、Square、GitHub及客服郵件服務依其實際角色處理相關請求或資料，可能另行處理服務連線及診斷資訊。以上技術說明不表示App完全不收集資料，也不表示已核實所有後端、第三方或營運端的記錄、轉送及其他用途均不存在；Soulcool不出售個人資料，也不為廣告用途將個人資料提供給廣告商。

4. Owner帳號刪除與處理限制

Owner可在App內「設定 → 帳號設定 → 刪除帳號」進入既有流程，並提供目前密碼完成本人驗證。現有Owner帳號刪除流程包含帳號使用者資料匿名化、Session撤銷、相關憑證刪除，以及部分店家顯示資料清理。這不表示所有可識別欄位、頭像、預約、營業紀錄、備份或第三方副本均已完整刪除，也不保證匿名化後不可重新識別；不能僅憑登入失效判定全部資料已清除。
若顯示部分失敗，或因逾時、回應遺失而無法確認結果，請勿重複提交，請聯絡客服確認。登入失效或App本機防重送標記不等於完整刪除的權威證據。本政策不表示帳號刪除會立即清除所有資料。若需要了解個別要求的進度與範圍，請聯絡客服。

5. 個人資料、營業紀錄與第三方副本

Soulcool的保存原則，是僅為營運、記帳、對帳、結算、適用法律義務及必要客服用途，保存所需資料。資料應僅限授權人員存取，並定期審查保存必要性；不再具有保存目的、且無其他適用保存義務的資料，依核准程序處理。
個人帳號清理與必要營業紀錄應分別處理，不因單一帳號要求而刪除其他員工資料或共用店家資料。保留範圍依實際用途、適用依據與未結事項判定；本政策不設定統一保存天數、不推定全部永久保留，也不表示已實施自動刪除機制。
Google、Square、備份、匯出及客服郵件服務可能持有不同責任範圍的資料或副本。Soulcool受理要求時應確認自己可處理與由第三方管理的範圍，向申請者說明相關安排及限制；App內帳號處理不表示同步永久清除所有外部副本，也不表示外部服務不會保存服務或診斷紀錄。

6. Employee及其他資料要求

使用者，包括Employee，可透過support@soulcoolapp.com提出資料存取、更正或刪除要求，也可就相關店家紀錄聯絡店家。這是資料要求受理管道，不表示已提供Employee在App內自助刪除功能。
Soulcool應核實申請者身分及資料歸屬，確認可刪除、需保留及由第三方管理的資料範圍，向申請者說明實際處理安排，並在確認完成後回覆。處理結果未確認前，不宣稱刪除成功，也不引導盲目重複提交；本政策不承諾未經確認的處理天數或後端自動結果查詢。
請提供問題描述與必要的帳號辨識資訊，不要寄送密碼、PIN或Session Token。員工帳號的停用或管理操作，不等於相關個資已完整刪除。

Soulcool Daily Multi Privacy Policy
Operator: Soulcool
Effective date: 2026-10-22

1. Scope and purposes

This policy applies to Soulcool Daily Multi and related support services. We process store and user identifiers, names, roles, account status, authentication information, and sessions for sign-in, identity verification, and account management. Enabled features also process employee information, optional employee avatars, appointment and design images, appointments, customer names and notes, service amounts, discounts, tips, payment allocations, daily income, performance, and settlement information for salon operations, checkout, reports, and reconciliation. Information voluntarily supplied to support is used to respond to and resolve requests.

2. Employee accounts and access

Owners create and manage Employee accounts. Employees sign in with individual account information and a PIN. Owners may maintain bookkeeping records, perform checkout, manage employees, and view reports. Employees may only view authorized information and may not create, modify, or delete bookkeeping records. Salons should ensure that information entry and access authorization are appropriate.

3. Google, Square, the website, and data use

The App sends requests needed for authentication, sessions, employee management, appointments, images, bookkeeping, checkout, and reports to its Google Apps Script backend. The existing service uses Google Sheets for salon information. When a salon connects Square, the App can display authorized appointments and related operational information. Recording payment amounts and payment methods does not mean that the App itself processes payment-card transactions.
The current App does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, implement IDFA/IDFV-based advertising identification, or implement cross-app advertising tracking. No App-initiated analytics-event upload was identified. The App has local diagnostic logging; local logs are not equivalent to remotely uploaded analytics events.
The official website is served through GitHub Pages. Opening the privacy policy connects the browser to that website; the App link does not attach account information, Store IDs, PINs, or session tokens. The current public home, privacy, and support pages do not embed identified advertising or analytics trackers. The home page script only updates the footer year.
Apple provides App distribution and applicable testing services. Google, Square, GitHub, and support email services handle requests or information according to their actual roles and may separately process connection and diagnostic information. These technical statements do not mean that the App collects no data, or establish that all backend, third-party, or operational logging, onward sharing, or other uses are absent. Soulcool does not sell personal information or provide personal information to advertisers for advertising purposes.

4. Owner account deletion and limitations

Owners can enter the existing flow through Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account and verify their identity with their current password. The existing Owner account-deletion flow includes anonymization of account-user information, session revocation, deletion of associated credentials, and cleanup of some store display information. This does not establish complete deletion of every identifying field, avatar, appointment, business record, backup, or external copy, nor does it establish that anonymized information cannot be reidentified. Loss of login access alone does not establish that all information has been erased.
If processing partially fails, or a timeout or lost response leaves the outcome unknown, do not resubmit; contact support to establish the result. Loss of login access or a local resubmission block is not authoritative evidence of complete deletion. Account deletion does not mean immediate removal of every item of information. Contact support for the status and scope of an individual request.

5. Personal information, business records, and external copies

Soulcool's retention policy is to keep only information needed for operations, bookkeeping, reconciliation, settlement, applicable legal obligations, and necessary support. Access should be restricted to authorized personnel, and retention needs should be reviewed periodically. Information with no remaining retention purpose or other applicable retention obligation is handled under approved procedures.
Personal-account cleanup is distinct from necessary business records. One account's request does not authorize deletion of other employees' information or shared salon information. Retention scope is determined by actual purposes, applicable grounds, and unresolved matters. This policy sets no single retention period, does not presume indefinite retention of all information, and does not represent automatic deletion as implemented.
Google, Square, backups, exports, and support email services may hold information or copies under different responsibilities. When handling a request, Soulcool should establish what it can process and what is managed by third parties, and explain the arrangements and limitations. In-app account processing does not establish simultaneous permanent erasure of every external copy, or mean that external services keep no service or diagnostic records.

6. Employee and other data requests

Users, including Employees, may request access, correction, or deletion through support@soulcoolapp.com, and may contact the relevant salon about its records. This request channel does not represent an in-app Employee self-service deletion feature as implemented.
Soulcool should verify the requester's identity and ownership of the information, determine what can be deleted, what must be retained, and what is managed by third parties, explain the actual processing arrangements, and respond after completion has been confirmed. Before the outcome is established, Soulcool must not claim successful deletion or encourage blind resubmission. This policy promises neither an unverified processing deadline nor an automatic backend result-query capability.
Provide an issue description and necessary account-identifying information, not passwords, PINs, or session tokens. Deactivation or management of an Employee account does not establish complete deletion of related personal information.
